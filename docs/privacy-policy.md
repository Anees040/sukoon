## The short version

Sukoon does not collect, store on servers, sell, or share any personal data.
Everything the app knows lives only on your phone.

## What the app stores — on your device only

- **Prayer log & streaks** — which prayers you marked prayed/jama'at/missed.
- **Qaza ledger** — the estimate inputs you confirmed and your repayment log.
- **Settings** — language, calculation method, silence window, reminders,
  favorite cities.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics SDKs, and no ads.

## Location

If you grant location permission, your coordinates are used **on the device**
to compute prayer times and qibla direction. Location is never transmitted
anywhere. You can instead pick a city manually — the app works fully offline
with its built-in Pakistani city list.

## Do Not Disturb access

Sukoon asks for Do Not Disturb access for exactly one purpose: silencing
your phone at prayer times you enabled, and restoring it afterwards. The app
never reads your notifications and never changes other DND settings.

## Permissions summary

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Do Not Disturb access | Silence at azan, restore after |
| Exact alarms | Fire silence exactly at prayer time |
| Notifications | "Phone is silent" status + optional reminders |
| Location (optional) | On-device prayer times & qibla |
| Ignore battery optimizations (optional) | Keep alarms reliable on aggressive battery savers |

## Children

Sukoon is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect any
data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds any data collection (e.g. opt-in crash
reports), this policy will be updated first and the change highlighted in
the app's update notes.
