## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Sukoon (سکون) silences your phone at every azan — automatically — and hands
it back after prayer. No more ringtone embarrassment in the masjid, no more
forgetting to un-silence afterwards.

Built for Pakistan, in English and اردو, fully offline.

◆ AUTO-SILENT AT PRAYER TIMES
• Phone goes silent exactly at azan, restores after 15/20/30 minutes
• Choose which prayers auto-silence
• Masjid mode: one tap = instant silence for your chosen window
• Survives restarts — set it once, forget it
• Status notification with “End now” so you’re always in control

◆ NAMAZ TRACKER
• One tap to log each prayer: prayed, with jama’at, or missed
• Streaks that motivate, a monthly heatmap that tells the truth
• Excused days for sisters — your streak stays safe

◆ QAZA-E-UMRI CALCULATOR & TRACKER
• Answer a few gentle questions → get an honest estimate of missed prayers
• Three estimation modes, including the cautious option
• Deductions for menstruation and post-natal days, calculated respectfully
• Repay day by day with a realistic plan (even 1 set a day finishes — the
  app shows you the date)
• Progress rings, milestones and achievements to keep you going
• Every number is an estimate — the app always says so, and shows its math

◆ QIBLA FINDER
• Compass with calibration guidance and a no-sensor fallback
• Accurate bearing computed on your device

◆ PRAYER TIMES FOR PAKISTAN
• 100+ cities offline — Karachi to Skardu, AJK included
• University of Karachi method with Hanafi Asr by default (adjustable)
• Optional pre-azan heads-up notification

◆ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No ads. No analytics. Nothing leaves your phone.

Permissions: Do Not Disturb access (to silence & restore), exact alarms
(fire at azan precisely), notifications (status + reminders), optional
location (on-device prayer times & qibla).

Sukoon — آپ کی نمازوں کا ساتھی۔
```
